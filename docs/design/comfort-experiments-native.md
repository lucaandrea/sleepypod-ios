# Native comfort comparisons

Mode: Operate. This extends the current web comfort-comparison workflow into the
existing native account and comfort screens. It inherits Theme, Form, system
navigation, disclosure controls, Dynamic Type and the established light/dark
appearances. No replacement visual identity or concept round is needed.

## Task and first viewport

A sleeper compares two nearby overnight settings without altering wake warming
or vibration. The first viewport explains that task and presents saved plans.
An empty library leads to a first-night date and nearby setting. A saved plan
opens with its two temperatures, current state and a prominent Stop action.
The owner and personal-account entry points use their respective credentials.

## Interaction contract

Plan review and permission to control the bed are distinct, expiring actions.
The control review names all affected bed zones, the temperature pair and the
preserved wake behavior. Its signature interaction is this explicit transition
from a saved comparison to a separately reviewed, scheduled experiment.
An interrupted write reveals Check saved result; reconnect never repeats it.
Stop closes the plan before cancelling its retained control permission. A
partially completed Stop remains visible and recoverable. Firmware readback
resolves uncertainty without pretending that earlier commands were delivered.

## Content and states

Use the real versioned Core schemas and account capability checks. Show actual
scheduled nights, settling versus comparison nights, wake boundaries, usable
observations and saved morning feedback. Errors, unavailable qualification,
loading, empty, pending, ended and expired states retain a meaningful next step.
Synthetic simulator examples are visibly labelled and cannot contact hardware.

## Visual constraints

Keep technical identifiers and raw enums out of the main task. Use restrained
secondary text and existing warning/destructive colors. Support the largest
accessibility text, iPhone and iPad portrait, and iPad landscape without truncating
actions or requiring horizontal scrolling. Wake controls remain separate.
No HTML/CSS detector applies to this native surface; native screenshots and an
independent finish review provide the visual check.

## Acceptance

Contract/transport tests use fixtures generated by the real isolated Core
planning service. Workflow tests cover account changes, expiry, lost replies,
partial Stop, recovery and background privacy. UI tests exercise plan/save,
review/authorize/Stop, errors, feedback and large text on phone and tablet.
Rendered captures and final review outcomes will be recorded after verification.
These checks do not demonstrate physical delivery or improved sleep outcomes.
