# Watch companion

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

This product record covers the native watchOS companion within the Apple app workspace.

## Users

The current sleeper needs quick bed controls without navigating the iPhone app at night.
The approved household plan adds independent people and their assigned sides; solo use
continues until the reviewed arrangement changes. A Watch must follow its paired
phone's current account and explicit bed scope.

## Product Purpose

Make temperature, Stop and the effective next wake easy to reach on the wrist.
Retain the existing optional research movement recorder as a secondary destination.

## Operating Context

The approved platform work requests everyday Watch controls, aligned with the ESP32
and widgets. This Watch uses a reachable paired iPhone for authenticated Pod access.
It does not promise independent cellular control. Commands must expire rather than
replay after reconnect. The iPhone may be locked after its first unlock since restart.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The implementation distinguishes requested water temperature, reported water readings,
controller acknowledgments and physical behavior. Water is not body temperature.
The initial Watch version reads the next wake and stops vibration; editing a wake
continues on iPhone. Missing or expired data never becomes a fresh measurement.
Private credentials remain on iPhone. Health and model keys retain their own protection.
No successful software test proves movement, warming, vibration or better sleep.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the existing sleepypod name and native Apple behavior. The approved experience
prioritizes simple, understandable nighttime controls and a prominent Stop action.

## Evidence on Hand

Shared command and account tests, source contracts and clearly labeled synthetic
Watch simulator examples. Physical phone/Watch delivery is a separate acceptance step.

## Product Principles

- Make the selected person and physical side explicit.
- Keep ordinary controls usable with large text and one-handed operation.
- Never repeat an uncertain actuator request.
- Show unavailable or stale information honestly.
